Communicate

This is a percussion quartet that I wrote in order to express the downfall of communication skills that I observed in society as a whole. In order to represent this lack of communication I used different accent patterns and overlapped them between the different parts. I wanted to have a focus on the types of cliques that exist, and so I separated the parts into 2 different groups. I then have them trade playing in the middle of the piece while the other group continues to play the accent patterns. I finally have the two groups come together and play a melody all together. This is to exemplify the idea of the joy that occurs when communication finally happens. I really enjoyed this project, and it pushed me to think outside of the box when it came to the rhythm. I liked the challenge of taking an abstract idea and transforming it into music.
