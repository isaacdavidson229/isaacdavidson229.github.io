Meaning of Light

This is a game that was created with a group of 2 other students for one of my classes. This is the music that would play on the title screen. I choose to use a music box soundfount for the music because the music box is an item in the game that determins whether the light acts as a particle or a wave. I wanted the music to be calm and relaxing, so I wrote the music in major, and made the tempo slow. This gives it the effect that the player is listening to an actual music box, and leads nicely into the start of the game.

This is the music that you would listen to when light acts like a particle in the game. I wanted the music to feel jagged to imply that light was rigid, and unmoving. Almost as though it was adearing to the limitations of a particle. I also gave the music a swing feel in order to make it sound more bouncy to add to the sharpness of the music. This piece is also the inverse of the wave theme. I did this because I wanted the two different themes to be connected in some way, and since light can act like either a particle or wave, I wanted to express that through the music. I change the style to straight eigthnotes in order to make it sound less choppy, and more flowing like a wave. It was a challenge to make the music in this way because I had to contantly be thinking about how it would sound in retrograde all while thinking about it normally as well.

Using the soundfont to get the musicbox sound was something that I had never done before this prroject, and I had to learn how to integrate the soundfont with my music writing software MuseScore. On top of that I also had to implement the music shift whenever the player pressed the music box inside of our game. I really enjoyed this project, and I enjoyed that it allowed me to stretch myself farther that I would have otherwise.
